ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Version 3.2

================================================================================
SECTION 1: EMPLOYMENT TERMS
================================================================================

1.1 At-Will Employment
All employment at Acme Corporation is "at-will," meaning that either the employee
or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or
without cause, and with or without notice, subject to the notice period
requirements outlined in Section 1.3.

1.2 Probationary Period
New employees are subject to a probationary period of ninety (90) calendar days
from the date of hire. During this period, performance will be evaluated, and
employment may be terminated without the standard notice period. Benefits
eligibility begins on the first day following successful completion of the
probationary period.

1.3 Notice Period
Employees wishing to resign must provide written notice as follows:
  - Junior staff (Grade 1–3): Thirty (30) calendar days
  - Mid-level staff (Grade 4–6): Sixty (60) calendar days
  - Senior staff (Grade 7–9): Ninety (90) calendar days
  - Executive leadership: One hundred twenty (120) calendar days

The company reserves the right to waive any portion of the notice period or
provide pay in lieu of notice at its discretion.

1.4 Working Hours
Standard working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a
one-hour lunch break. The standard work week is forty (40) hours. Overtime must
be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager and is compensated at 1.5 times
the regular hourly rate for non-exempt employees.

================================================================================
SECTION 2: COMPENSATION & BENEFITS
================================================================================

2.1 Salary Structure
Salaries are reviewed annually during the performance review cycle in April.
Adjustments are based on individual performance ratings, market benchmarking,
and company financial performance. The company targets the 60th percentile
of industry compensation for all roles.

2.2 Annual Bonus
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus of up to:
  - Grade 1–3: 10% of annual base salary
  - Grade 4–6: 15% of annual base salary
  - Grade 7–9: 20% of annual base salary
  - Executive: 25–40% of annual base salary
Bonus payments are made in May following the performance year.

2.3 Health Insurance
The company provides comprehensive health insurance coverage for all full-time
employees and their dependents. The plan includes:
  - Medical coverage (PPO and HMO options)
  - Dental coverage (preventive and major)
  - Vision coverage (annual eye exam and lens/frame allowance)
  - Mental health and wellness support
Employee contribution is 20% of the premium; the company covers 80%.
Coverage begins on the first of the month following 30 days of employment.

2.4 Retirement Plan
Acme Corporation offers a 401(k) retirement plan with company matching.
The company matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary,
and 50% of contributions between 4% and 6% of salary, for a maximum
company match of 5% of annual salary.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: LEAVE POLICIES
================================================================================

3.1 Paid Time Off (PTO)
PTO accrues based on years of service:
  - 0–2 years: 15 days per year
  - 3–5 years: 20 days per year
  - 6–10 years: 25 days per year
  - 10+ years: 30 days per year
PTO may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 unused days. Unused PTO beyond
the carryover limit will be forfeited on December 31st of each year.

3.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive ten (10) paid sick days per calendar year. Sick leave does
not carry over year to year. A medical certificate is required for absences
exceeding three (3) consecutive working days.

3.3 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to twenty-six (26) weeks of paid maternity leave
at full salary. Maternity leave may begin up to four (4) weeks before the
expected delivery date. The employee must provide written notice at least
sixty (60) days prior to the planned start of maternity leave.

3.4 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to four (4) weeks of paid paternity leave at full
salary, to be taken within six (6) months of the child's birth or adoption.

3.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to five (5) paid days of bereavement leave for the death
of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, sibling). Three (3) paid
days are provided for extended family members.

3.6 Public Holidays
The company observes twelve (12) public holidays per calendar year, as published
annually by the Human Resources department.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
================================================================================

4.1 Professional Behaviour
All employees are expected to conduct themselves in a professional manner.
Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or any form of hostile workplace behaviour
will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

4.2 Confidentiality
Employees must maintain strict confidentiality regarding proprietary company
information, client data, trade secrets, and internal processes. This obligation
survives the termination of employment for a period of two (2) years.

4.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or business interests that
could conflict with their duties at Acme Corporation. Undisclosed conflicts
may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

4.4 Anti-Bribery and Corruption
Acme Corporation has a zero-tolerance policy for bribery and corruption.
Employees must not offer, promise, give, or accept any bribe or improper
advantage, whether directly or through intermediaries.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES
================================================================================

5.1 Progressive Discipline
The company follows a progressive discipline approach:
  Step 1: Verbal warning (documented in personnel file)
  Step 2: Written warning
  Step 3: Final written warning with performance improvement plan (PIP)
  Step 4: Termination of employment

5.2 Grounds for Immediate Termination
The following offences may result in termination without progressive discipline:
  - Theft, fraud, or embezzlement
  - Workplace violence or threats of violence
  - Gross insubordination
  - Possession or use of illegal substances on company premises
  - Serious breach of the confidentiality policy
  - Sexual harassment

================================================================================
SECTION 6: GRIEVANCE RESOLUTION
================================================================================

6.1 Informal Resolution
Employees are encouraged to first attempt to resolve workplace concerns
informally by speaking directly with their manager or HR Business Partner.

6.2 Formal Grievance Process
If informal resolution is unsuccessful, employees may submit a formal
written grievance to the Head of Human Resources within thirty (30) days
of the incident. HR will investigate and provide a written response within
fifteen (15) working days.

6.3 Appeal
If the employee is dissatisfied with the outcome, they may appeal in writing
to the Chief People Officer within ten (10) working days of receiving the
decision. The appeal decision is final and binding.
