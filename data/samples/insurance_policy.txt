GLOBALSHIELD INSURANCE COMPANY
COMPREHENSIVE HOME INSURANCE POLICY
Policy Number: GS-HOME-2025-SAMPLE
Effective Date: January 1, 2025

================================================================================
PART A: DEFINITIONS
================================================================================

In this policy, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned below:

"Insured" means the policyholder named in the schedule and any person permanently
residing in the insured property who is a family member of the policyholder.

"Property" means the building and its permanent fixtures at the address stated
in the policy schedule, including garages, outbuildings, walls, gates, and fences
forming part of the property.

"Contents" means household goods, personal belongings, and furniture owned by
the Insured and located within the Property, excluding motor vehicles and
business equipment.

"Sum Insured" means the maximum amount payable under this policy for any single
claim or aggregate of claims during the policy period.

"Excess" (also known as "Deductible") means the amount that the Insured must pay
toward each claim before the insurer's liability begins. The standard excess under
this policy is $500 per claim.

"Accidental Damage" means physical damage caused by a sudden, unexpected, and
unintended event that is not otherwise excluded under this policy.

================================================================================
PART B: COVERAGE
================================================================================

SECTION 1: Building Coverage

1.1 Perils Covered
This policy covers loss or damage to the Property caused by:
  (a) Fire, smoke, lightning, and explosion
  (b) Storm, flood, and water damage (subject to flood endorsement)
  (c) Theft or attempted theft, with evidence of forced entry
  (d) Vandalism and malicious damage
  (e) Impact by vehicles or aircraft
  (f) Subsidence, heave, or landslip (subject to sub-limit)
  (g) Burst or leaking water pipes and fixed heating systems
  (h) Falling trees, branches, or aerials

1.2 Sum Insured — Building
The building is insured for its full rebuilding cost as stated in the
policy schedule, up to a maximum of $750,000 unless a higher limit
is endorsed.

SECTION 2: Contents Coverage

2.1 Perils Covered
Contents are covered against the same perils as listed in Section 1.1,
plus accidental damage if the Accidental Damage Extension is purchased.

2.2 Sum Insured — Contents
Contents are insured up to the amount stated in the policy schedule.
The standard limit is $150,000. Individual high-value items (jewellery,
art, electronics exceeding $2,500 per item) must be individually listed
in the schedule.

2.3 Accidental Damage Extension
If purchased, this extension covers sudden and accidental physical damage
to Contents, including:
  - Spills, drops, and breakage of personal electronics
  - Accidental tearing or burning of furniture and furnishings
  - Damage caused by children or pets
The accidental damage excess is $250 per claim.

SECTION 3: Personal Liability

3.1 Coverage
This policy includes personal liability coverage up to $1,000,000 per
occurrence. This protects the Insured against legal liability for bodily
injury to third parties or damage to third-party property arising from
incidents at or originating from the Property.

3.2 Exclusions from Liability
Liability coverage does not extend to:
  (a) Injuries to members of the Insured's household
  (b) Damage to property owned or rented by the Insured
  (c) Liability arising from professional or business activities
  (d) Liability arising from ownership of motor vehicles or watercraft

================================================================================
PART C: EXCLUSIONS
================================================================================

This policy does NOT cover loss, damage, or liability caused by or arising from:

1. Wear and tear, gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, or mould
2. Faulty or defective design, materials, or workmanship
3. Mechanical or electrical breakdown (unless resulting from an insured peril)
4. War, terrorism, civil commotion, or nuclear contamination
5. Intentional acts or criminal conduct by the Insured
6. Damage occurring while the Property is unoccupied for more than sixty (60)
   consecutive days, unless prior written approval has been obtained from
   the insurer
7. Losses arising from cyber incidents, data breaches, or digital attacks
8. Earth movement, including earthquake, unless specifically endorsed
9. Government action, including confiscation, nationalisation, or demolition
   by order of any public authority

================================================================================
PART D: CLAIMS PROCESS
================================================================================

SECTION 1: Reporting a Claim

1.1 Notification
The Insured must notify GlobalShield Insurance of any claim or potential claim
within forty-eight (48) hours of the discovery of the loss or damage by calling
the 24-hour claims hotline at 1-800-555-CLAIM or submitting a claim online
at www.globalshield.example.com/claims.

1.2 Police Report
For claims involving theft, vandalism, or malicious damage, the Insured must
file a police report within twenty-four (24) hours and provide the report
number to GlobalShield Insurance.

1.3 Documentation Required
The following documents must be submitted with every claim:
  (a) Completed claim form
  (b) Proof of ownership (receipts, photos, valuations)
  (c) Repair estimates from at least two qualified contractors
  (d) Police report number (for theft/vandalism claims)
  (e) Photographs of the damage

SECTION 2: Claim Assessment

2.1 Inspection
GlobalShield Insurance reserves the right to inspect the Property and any
damaged items before authorising repairs or settlement.

2.2 Settlement Basis
  - Building claims are settled on a "new for old" reinstatement basis
  - Contents claims are settled on an "indemnity" basis (current market
    value), unless the New Replacement Value endorsement has been purchased
  - Depreciation will be applied to items more than five (5) years old
    unless covered by the New Replacement Value endorsement

2.3 Payment Timeline
Once a claim is approved, payment will be made within fifteen (15) business
days via electronic funds transfer to the bank account on file.

SECTION 3: Dispute Resolution

3.1 Internal Review
If the Insured disagrees with a claim decision, they may request an internal
review by writing to the Claims Manager within thirty (30) days of the decision.

3.2 External Mediation
If the dispute is not resolved internally, either party may refer the matter
to an independent mediator. The costs of mediation will be shared equally
between the parties.

3.3 Arbitration
As a final recourse, disputes may be submitted to binding arbitration in
accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association. The
arbitration award shall be final and enforceable in any court of competent
jurisdiction.

================================================================================
PART E: GENERAL CONDITIONS
================================================================================

1. Duty of Disclosure — The Insured must disclose all material facts that may
   affect the insurer's decision to accept the risk or set the premium.

2. Reasonable Precautions — The Insured must take all reasonable steps to
   prevent loss or damage and to maintain the Property in good condition.

3. Other Insurance — If the Insured has other insurance covering the same risk,
   GlobalShield Insurance will only pay its rateable proportion of the claim.

4. Cancellation — Either party may cancel this policy by providing thirty (30)
   days' written notice. If cancelled by the Insured, a pro-rata refund of
   the unearned premium will be provided.

5. Policy Renewal — This policy is renewable annually. Premium adjustments
   may apply based on claims history, property valuation changes, and
   market conditions.
